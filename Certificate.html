🎓 Download Your CPD Certificate / अपना CPD प्रमाणपत्र डाउनलोड करें

Enter your 13-digit EmployeeCodeDOB (5-digit Employee Code followed by Date of Birth in DDMMYYYY format). An OTP will be sent only to your registered email address. WhatsApp and SMS OTP are not used.

Your certificate is retrieved by matching your verified participant name with the certificate filename in the designated CPD Certificates Drive folder. If a matching file is missing or ambiguous, no certificate will be released.
